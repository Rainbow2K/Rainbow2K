### 🚀 What I'm building

- **[SVC Systems](https://svcsystems.eu)** · Digital Product Passport infrastructure for fashion & beauty. One place that aggregates supplier and manufacturer data, maps the supply chain and builds the passport required by the EU ESPR regulation.
- **[Mały Matematyk](https://github.com/Rainbow2K/maly-matematyk)** · A 135M–1.5B parameter LLM running fully in the browser (WebGPU) that turns math and physics problems into equations, while a numerical solver computes and verifies the answer.
- **[Streamlore](https://github.com/Rainbow2K/streamlore)** · Collect shared Twitch moments as animated, non-transferable cards.
- **[Sailio](https://sailio.app)** · Tools for yacht charter companies.
